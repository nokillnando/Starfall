--[[
NOTES:
    Cargo vehicles are not fully supported, suspension wise, with tunning the settings you can get something close to that
    which will hold some cargo but real simulation of those are not supported(yet), so heavy duty cargo stuff is off and
    untested at best

    While for the most part you can get 90% realistic results some times are obviously missing and may or may not be planned
    such as hybrid and electric vehicles
    
    Many of the features are untested at best(dont have test beds for those), such as turbo and charger
    
    This chip has a auto update feature(can be disabled below), which as long as the config is on the required version of the core,
    it'll auto update to the newest core on spawn, for the updated cores only. If not or offline, it'll use local versions, if you disable it as well.
    
    Tire physics while they are mostly realistic you can tune them to whatever you feel best for the car.
    
    There are bound to have bugs in it, due to the nature of the whole thing its impossible to test each and every combination possible.
    Whenever those occur, do contact me at: https://steamcommunity.com/id/nokillnando/
    Also make sure to provide a dupe for it, or I wont be able to test and fix the bug
    
    YES THIS IS MADE WITH AI, don't like it? Not my problem, don't use it and don't bother me either, see it for the end result not the means.
    
    
    Changelog:
        R4:
         New Boost settings, for when theres only the boosted specs available
         Better climbing gear hold system
         Better optimization overall up to 45% less, do contact me if something feels wrong or bugged though.
         New Config Schema with better organization overall   
        
        R3:
         Initial public release, needs player testing, releases together with the Peugeot 106 S16 dupe
]]--
